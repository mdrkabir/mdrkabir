## Selected research

### Behind Harmful Compliance: Behavioral and Mechanistic Divergence Across LLM Jailbreaks

*Preprint · 2026 · First author*

Compared three post-training interventions and found distinct changes in capabilities, safety judgments, and internal representations even when harmful compliance looked similar.

[Paper ↗](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18510)

### Deep Reinforcement Learning with Time-Scale Invariant Memory

*AAAI · 2025 · First author*

Integrated time-scale invariant memory into deep reinforcement learning agents, improving robustness across temporal scales compared with recurrent-memory baselines.

[Paper ↗](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/32124) · [Code ↗](https://github.com/cogneuroai/RL-with-scale-invariant-memory)

### Path integration impairments reveal early cognitive changes in subjective cognitive decline

*Science Advances · 2025 · Co-author*

Used hierarchical Bayesian modeling to analyze path-integration behavior in an immersive virtual-reality study of subjective cognitive decline.

[Paper ↗](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adw6404) · [Code ↗](https://github.com/cogneuroai/Bayesian-hierarchical-model-for-PI)

## Methods & tools

My work combines model training and evaluation, temporal decision-making, and probabilistic inference. I primarily use Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, JAX, NumPyro, and Slurm.
