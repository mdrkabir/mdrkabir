# Editing guide

- Edit `README.md` to change links, bio text, and section order.
- Replace `README.md` with `README-static.md` if you want a non-animated header.
- Header graphics are in `assets/console-*.gif` and `assets/console-*-static.png`.
- Research cards are `assets/card-01-*.png`, `card-02-*.png`, and `card-03-*.png`.
- Section heading graphics are `assets/heading-research-*.png` and `assets/heading-tools-*.png`.
