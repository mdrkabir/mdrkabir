# Stack logo attribution

The PNGs in `stack/` provide light and dark versions of the profile's 26 tool
logos. Product names and marks belong to their respective owners.

## Source-derived artwork

- Most tool outlines retain [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons)
  geometry from the original profile assets, with some adapted colors.
- Matplotlib retains artwork sourced from the
  [Matplotlib project](https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib).
- verl retains an interpretation of the
  [project organization avatar](https://github.com/verl-project).
- vLLM uses the two-path V mark from
  [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/src/Vllm/components/Color.tsx).
  Its MIT notice is in [licenses/LOBE-ICONS.txt](licenses/LOBE-ICONS.txt).
- MATLAB uses [theSVG's vector rendition](https://thesvg.org/icon/matlab),
  retaining its geometry, gradients, and colors. Its MIT notice is in
  [licenses/THESVG.txt](licenses/THESVG.txt).

## Reconstructed and representative marks

These are not all unmodified official logos:

- JAX is a reconstruction of its
  [colored-letter mark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jax-ml/jax/main/images/jax_logo_250px.png).
- NumPyro is a reconstruction of its
  [Pyro monogram](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pyro-ppl/numpyro/master/docs/source/_static/img/pyro_logo.png).
- Slurm/HPC and SQL use representative cluster and database symbols.
- Bash/Zsh uses Bash-related artwork; Seaborn uses a simplified wave mark.
